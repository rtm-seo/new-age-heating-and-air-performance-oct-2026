01Executive summary

This report covers Google Search Console and Google Analytics performance for newageheatingandair.com from July 1, 2025 to October 27, 2026. Over that period the site earned 965 clicks from 421,550 impressions (0.23% CTR) at an average position of 25.9.

Year over year, Q3 2026 delivered 57% more clicks than Q3 2025 (268 vs 171) and CTR nearly doubled, with the sharpest ranking gains after the June 2026 optimization. Since the Q4 2025 low, impressions have grown every quarter. About 87% of clicks attributable to a query come from brand searches.

In Google Analytics, the site recorded 12,952 sessions over the same period. Organic search sessions rose 44% year over year (Q3 2025 vs Q3 2026) while paid search fell 82%, and organic search generated 45.5% of all key events from 15.9% of sessions.

Call tracking recorded 742 calls over the period, 447 of them qualified. With every tracking number live, calls more than doubled from Q2 to Q3 2026 (158 to 348). Website calls have the highest quality of any source (82% qualified), and the website produced 178 tracked leads (calls and forms) since April 2026. Website lead tracking stopped from August 26 to early October 2026, as covered in section 04.

Section 05 sets out three SEO recommendations (expanding the city + service pages, strengthening the core service pages and adding missing ones, and improving click-through from search results) and two website updates to increase conversions (a short quote form on the homepage and main service and city pages, and trust signals next to each call to action).

4Website lead tracking stopped from August 26 to early October 2026

Daily Google Analytics data shows the last form submission on August 25, 2026. From August 26 to September 29, the site recorded no form starts, form submissions, phone clicks or thank-you events in Google Analytics, while page views kept recording normally. Form starts returned on September 30, phone clicks on October 3 and form submissions on October 5. Google Analytics and Tag Manager were not the cause. The website itself broke: in late August the pages stopped rendering their content and showed raw page-builder code instead (as seen on the homepage on August 29), so the contact form and the phone links inside the page content did not load. The site header, and the page tracking in it, kept working, which is why page views continued while form and phone activity dropped to zero.

05Recommendations

SEO recommendations

1

Move city + service pages onto page one

High priority

City + service searches are the largest demand pool (172,583 impressions in 13 months). The six core cities account for 88% of that demand, but their hub pages are the thinnest on the site and rank 20 to 34 on average. Eight cities with 13,073 AC and heating impressions have no city service page at all (see the tables under Search Console insight 4).

  • Expand the six core city pages to 900+ words of local content: /corona-ca/ (653 words), /norco-ca/ (356), /eastvale-ca/ (366), /chino-ca/ (363), /chino-hills-ca/ (356) and /riverside-ca/ (361).
  • Give each city page neighborhoods served, local housing and climate issues, a recent job example with photos, city-specific reviews and 4 to 5 FAQs with FAQPage schema.
  • Create AC and heating subpages for the eight cities that only have a hub page: Anaheim Hills, Yorba Linda, Fullerton, Jurupa Valley, Diamond Bar, Brea, Placentia and Mira Loma.
2

Strengthen the core service pages and add missing ones

High priority

Rankings broadened sharply, but the main service pages still earn almost no clicks, and several services customers search for have no page at all.

  • Expand each page with the repair process, common problems, typical price ranges, brands serviced, warranty terms and an FAQ block.
  • Add internal links to these pages from every city page and from related blog posts, using descriptive anchor text such as “AC repair in Corona”.

3

Turn better rankings into more clicks

High priority

Clicks are up 57% year over year and top-10 rankings grew 5.1×, but CTR is 0.26% overall. Search results need to give searchers a clearer reason to click.

  • Rewrite the title tags that run over 60 characters on the service pages and city service pages to the pattern “Service in City, CA | Business Name”, with the main service and city first.
  • Shorten the meta descriptions on pages where they run over 155 characters, and include a clear benefit and call to action (24/7 service, license number, free estimate).
4

Add a short quote form to the homepage and main service and city pages

High priority
